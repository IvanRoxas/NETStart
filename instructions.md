[Mini-Game 3A: Final AstroLink Repair]
HTML Concept: Hyperlinks and Anchors (<a>, href). The player must write the anchor tags and target the correct href URLs to establish a live network connection between Mars, Earth, and Venus, officially bringing the AstroLink back online.   Role & ObjectiveAct as the Lead UI/UX Frontend Developer. Build the React components, state logic, and animations for Mars Level 3. This level introduces a highly interactive, mechanical puzzle where writing HTML physically controls a massive sci-fi structure in the simulation panel.Core Gameplay Mechanics (The Leyline Cannon)This level abandons the "passive scan" mechanic. The player must actively build and fire the connections. The simulation responds in real-time to each piece of code added to the workspace.Mechanic 1: The Anchor (<a> </a>)Action: The player drags the [ Anchor Wrapper ] block into the workspace.Simulation Response: The AstroLink dish physically transforms. Heavy mechanical pylons slam into the Martian surface, locking the cannon into firing position.Mechanic 2: The Coordinates (href="...")Action: The player snaps the [ Target Attribute ] block onto the Anchor and selects a URL from the dropdown (e.g., earth.html).Simulation Response: The massive dish rotates on its axis, aiming through a holographic solar storm toward the selected target (Earth, Venus, or a decoy space rock).Mechanic 3: The Bridge (Text Label)Action: The player types text (e.g., "Connect Earth") inside the Anchor tags.Simulation Response: A glowing, clickable neon button bearing that exact text materializes on the AstroLink control terminal in the simulation UI.Mechanic 4: The Execution (Click to Fire)Action: The player uses their mouse to physically click the neon button they just coded on the terminal.Simulation Response: The cannon fires a brilliant blue energy tether.Pass & Fail States (Validation)The validation check happens on click of the generated link, not via a generic "Run" button.Fail State (Wrong href): If the player clicks a link wired to asteroid_belt.html, the blue tether fires but ricochets off a space rock. A buzzer sounds, the link flashes red, and the player must change the dropdown to try again.Pass State (Correct href): If the link is wired to earth.html, the tether pierces the storm and hooks onto Earth's receiver. The Earth hologram lights up green.Level Completion: The player must successfully anchor, aim, and fire tethers to both Earth and Venus. Once both holograms are green, the storm completely clears. Emma G. and Penny G. appear on screen to celebrate, and Oberion calls to direct the player to Venus. Edge Cases & Defensive ProgrammingTo ensure a frictionless educational experience, the simulation must gracefully handle edge cases without breaking the UI. Implement the following safeguards:Empty Text Labels: If the player leaves the [ Label ] input blank but sets the href, the neon button on the terminal should render as a glitching, unclickable static block with the warning "MISSING LINK TEXT". This teaches them that an <a> tag is invisible on a webpage without nested content.Duplicate Connections: The player must establish two distinct connections. If they wire two anchors to earth.html, the system should reject the second attempt with a "DUPLICATE SIGNAL: Target already connected" error, forcing them to route one specifically to Venus.Drag-and-Drop Limitations: Cap the workspace limit to exactly two [ Anchor ] block structures to prevent players from spamming the screen with infinite satellite pylons and crashing the React state.Audio & Sensory FeedbackBecause this level turns a line of code into a physical machine, the sound design must carry the weight of the interactions.Block Placement: Snapping the [ Anchor ] block should trigger a heavy, metallic "clank" and the low rumble of machinery deploying.Aiming: Changing the href dropdown should play a mechanical whirring sound (like a turret rotating).Execution & Validation:Pass: A high-frequency energy hum culminating in a satisfying, echoing laser blast, followed by a digital "success" chime.Fail: A sharp ricochet sound effect followed by a distinct error buzzer (e.g., "BZZZT - Signal Refused").Narrative & Dialogue HooksThe game state must trigger the exact storyline conclusions required for the HTML module finale.
